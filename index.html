About Our Institution

Our Mission

رسالتنا

To provide authentic Islamic education, foster personal growth, and serve the Muslim community with dedication and excellence. We strive to be a beacon of Islamic knowledge and values in Nigeria.

Our Vision

رؤيتنا

To be the leading center for Islamic education and Arabic studies in West Africa, producing scholars and leaders who will contribute positively to society while maintaining strong Islamic values.

About Our Mudir

Sheikh Dr. Abbas Abdulazeez

Sheikh Dr. Abbas Abdulazeez is the esteemed founder of Markaz-ul-Uloom School of Arabic and Islamic Studies, and a distinguished Islamic scholar, educationist, and community leader whose vision and scholarship have shaped Islamic education in Nigeria. With deep grounding in Arabic, Qur'anic studies, Hadith, and Islamic jurisprudence, he established Markaz-ul-Uloom over four decades ago, transforming it from a modest centre in Otubu, Agege into a renowned institution now headquartered in Alagbado.

Under his leadership, the Markaz has produced thousands of scholars and professionals, blending classical Islamic knowledge with contemporary relevance. Known for his humility, principled leadership, and community service, Sheikh Abbas remains a guiding force in the moral and intellectual development of countless individuals and the broader Muslim community.

Our History

تاريخنا

Founded in 1985, Markazul-Uloom has been a cornerstone of Islamic education in Alagbado, Lagos State, Nigeria. From humble beginnings with a single classroom, we have grown into a comprehensive Islamic educational institution serving thousands of students over four decades.

Frequently Asked Questions

الأسئلة الشائعة

Can't find your answer? Contact us and we'll be glad to help.

What does Markaz-ul-Uloom teach?

Quranic studies (memorization, Tajweed, Tafsir and Quranic Arabic) and a full Arabic language program covering grammar (Nahw), morphology (Sarf), classical literature and modern conversation. Our Academic Programmes page lists the subjects at each stage.

Who can join, and at what level?

We run structured programs for all ages. The Arabic language program starts at beginner level and goes up to advanced, and the curriculum is organised in four stages: Primary, Secondary, Advanced Studies and Teacher Training. Get in touch and the office will help you find the right level for you or your child.

How do I enrol?

Admissions and enrollment are handled by our Student Services office. Call or message us on WhatsApp at +234 814 531 8366, or email markazululoomalagbado40@gmail.com, and the team will explain the next steps.

WhatsApp messages are usually answered within 1 hour during office hours, and emails within 24 hours.

What are the fees, and is financial help available?

Current fees are not listed on this website, so please ask the office for up-to-date figures. Our Finance Office handles fee payments and financial assistance enquiries, and the school also runs scholarship programs for students.

When is the office open, and where can I find you?

Friday 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Saturday and Sunday 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM. We are at 1 Buyide Avenue, Alagbado, Lagos State, Nigeria. See the Contact page for all our addresses and ways to reach us.

Al-Uloom Central Mosque

مشروع المسجد

Expanding Our Sacred Space for the Community

Our Vision for the New Masjid

The Al-Uloom Central Mosque is an ambitious initiative to expand our current mosque facilities, creating a larger, more accommodating, and personally uplifting space for our growing community. Our vision is to build a masjid that not only serves as a place of worship but also as a vibrant center for education, community engagement, and personal development.

The expansion will include a larger prayer hall, dedicated spaces for women, enhanced ablution facilities, and additional classrooms for our Islamic studies programs. We aim to blend traditional Islamic architecture with modern amenities, ensuring comfort and functionality for all worshippers.

500+worshippers accommodated for daily prayers and special occasions
200+students in modern Islamic-studies classrooms

Why This Project Matters

  • Growing Community Needs: Our current facilities are becoming insufficient to accommodate the increasing number of worshippers and students.
  • Enhanced Learning Environment: New classrooms will allow us to expand our educational offerings and cater to more students.
  • Community Hub: The expanded masjid will serve as a central point for community gatherings, interfaith dialogues, and social services.
  • Spiritual Growth: A more spacious and beautiful environment will enhance the personal experience for all who visit.
