To provide authentic Islamic education, foster spiritual growth, and serve the Muslim community with dedication and excellence. We strive to be a beacon of Islamic knowledge and values in Nigeria.

رؤيتنا

To be the leading center for Islamic education and Arabic studies in West Africa, producing scholars and leaders who will contribute positively to society while maintaining strong Islamic values.

About Our Mudir

Sheikh Dr. Abbas Abdulazeez

Sheikh Dr. Abbas Abdulazeez is the esteemed founder of Markaz-ul-Uloom School of Arabic and Islamic Studies, and a distinguished Islamic scholar, educationist, and community leader whose vision and scholarship have shaped Islamic education in Nigeria.

With deep grounding in Arabic, Qur'anic studies, Hadith, and Islamic jurisprudence, he established Markaz-ul-Uloom over four decades ago, transforming it from a modest centre in Otubu, Agege into a renowned institution now headquartered in Alagbado.

Under his leadership, the Markaz has produced thousands of scholars and professionals, blending classical Islamic knowledge with contemporary relevance. Known for his humility, principled leadership, and community service, Sheikh Abbas remains a guiding force in the moral and intellectual development of countless individuals and the broader Muslim community.
